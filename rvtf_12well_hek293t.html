Step-by-Step (per well)

  1. 1
    Prepare Tube A — Lipofectamine mix
    Add 6.0 µL Lipofectamine 3000 to 100 µL Opti-MEM. Mix gently by pipetting.
  2. 2
    Prepare Tube B — DNA mix
    Combine 2.0 µg pDNA + 4.0 µL P3000 Reagent in 100 µL Opti-MEM. Mix gently.
  3. 3
    Combine A + B → form complexes
    Add Tube A contents to Tube B (200 µL total). Mix gently, incubate 15 min at RT.
  4. 4
    Add complexes to empty well first
    This is the reverse step — pipette complexes into the empty well before adding cells.
  5. 5
    Seed HEK293T cells on top
    Add 0.8 M HEK293T cells resuspended in 2.0 mL antibiotic-free growth media directly onto the complexes.
  6. 6
    Distribute and incubate
    Swirl plate gently. Incubate at 37 °C / 5% CO₂.
  7. 7
    Optional media change at 4–6 h
    Replace with complete growth media to reduce lipofection-associated toxicity. Harvest at 24–48 h post-transfection per your 48-well schedule.

Note: Reagent amounts run ~2× above Thermo Fisher's standard recommendation — this offset is inherited from the validated 48-well source protocol and is consistent across all formats. The higher cell density is appropriate for rvTF, where cells settle onto pre-formed complexes. If this is the first run at 12-well scale, consider a titration at 1.5 µg pDNA / 3.75 µL L3K / 3.0 µL P3K (closer to Thermo standard) to monitor toxicity alongside the scaled dose.
