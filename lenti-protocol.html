Day 0
Prepare & coat
0/1
Coat with poly-D-lysine — add 5 mL per T75 (or proportional volume), incubate 15 min, then aspirate and recycle.
Plates/flasks can be coated weeks ahead and stored at 4 °C.

0/2

Pool with Harvest #1 if concentrating, or store separately as Harvest #2 aliquots at −80 °C.
Conc.
Ultracentrifugation
0/4
Fill Ultraclear centrifuge tubes to ≤1 cm below the top. Top up with PBS if needed.
Spin at 20,000 g × 2 h at 4 °C = 10,500 rpm SURESPIN360.
Carefully aspirate supernatant. Resuspend pellet in 250–500 µL AdDMEM F12 + GlutaMAX + P/S + HEPES.
Aliquot 50–100 µL into labelled cryotubes. Freeze at −80 °C. Use at 1:100 dilution (concentrated).

Transduction notes
Day −1
Seed target cells
0/1
Seed 75,000 cells/well (MC38) or 150–200K/well (A549) in 6-well plate. Aim for 50–70% confluency at transduction.
Day 0
Transduction
0/4
Add virus per the plate plan above. Use 8 µg/mL Polybrene (1.6 µL of 10 mg/mL stock per 2 mL).
Add 2 mL infection medium per well. Test several dilutions (undiluted + 1:5 + 1:10 is a good start).
For MC38: 2 mL/well directly with polybrene. For A549: seed at 150–200K/well.
Incubate 4–8 h at 37 °C, then replace with fresh GM.
Add selection antibiotic 3 days post-transduction. Puromycin: 2 µg/mL (verify with kill curve).

Step 1
Dissociate organoids
0/5
Collect ~4 full Matrigel domes (20 µL each) per condition. Wash once with 3 mL ice-cold AdDMEM F12.
Leave on ice 5–10 min to dissolve Matrigel. Spin down, discard supernatant.
Resuspend in 1 mL pre-warmed TrypLE. Incubate 5–10 min at 37 °C.
Add 2 vol DMEM to quench TrypLE. Spin at 190 g, discard supernatant.
Resuspend in 2 mL infection medium per condition. Add virus.
Step 2
Spinoculation
0/3
Seed at high density in 2 wells of a 48-well plate (~500 µL per well).
Seal with parafilm. Spin 600 g × 1 h at 32 °C.
Transfer to incubator. Leave 3–4 h at 37 °C.
Step 3
Re-embed & culture
0/4
Collect cells, spin down, discard supernatant. Resuspend in 160 µL cold Matrigel.
Plate 8 × 20 µL domes in a 48-well plate. Invert 5 min, then incubate to polymerize.
Add 300 µL full medium + ROCK inhibitor (1×) per well.
Add selection 3 days post-transduction. Puromycin: 2 µg/mL. Hygromycin: 200 µM.
Or skip selection — grow and sort by fluorescence if GOI has a reporter.
